01 · ABOUT US

Three wings, one community

Bhargavi Housing Society is home to families across Wing A, Wing B and Wing C, built around a shared courtyard that has hosted everything from flag hoistings to festival feasts. Our managing committee — President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer and Joint Secretary — works with resident volunteers to keep the society safe, well-maintained and genuinely social.

Whether it's a rangoli competition, a safety workshop, or the Annual General Body Meeting, our courtyard has a way of turning 216 households into one neighbourhood.
