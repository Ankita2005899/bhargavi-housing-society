Government Schemes You May Be Eligible For

A quick, organised starting point to central government welfare schemes — grouped by caste & community, student status, income level, and age. Every "Apply / Learn more" button takes you straight to the real, official government page for that scheme.

🧭 Not sure which one applies to you?

Use the Government of India's own eligibility finder — answer a few questions about yourself and it matches you to schemes automatically.

Caste & Community Welfare

Scholarships, business support and welfare schemes for SC, ST, OBC and EWS communities. Documents typically needed: caste certificate, income certificate, Aadhaar.

🎓

PM-YASASVI (OBC / EBC / DNT students)

Full financial support for OBC, EBC and De-notified Tribe students for studies beyond Class XII, awarded through a national entrance test.

myscheme.gov.in Learn more ↗
📚

Pre & Post-Matric Scholarships (SC/ST/OBC)

Central scholarships covering tuition and related costs for SC, ST and OBC students from Class 1 through post-graduation, income-linked.

scholarships.gov.in — National Scholarship Portal Apply on official portal ↗
💼

Stand-Up India

Bank loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs setting up a new greenfield enterprise.

standupmitra.in Learn more ↗
🏘️

PM-AJAY (Village & community development)

Support for infrastructure, skill development and livelihood projects in villages and areas with a significant SC population.

myscheme.gov.in Search on official portal ↗
🎓

Students & Education

Scholarships, loans and skill-development support, open to students of every category. Most run through one of two national portals.

🗂️

National Scholarship Portal (NSP)

The single-window portal for most central and many state scholarships — search by class, category and course to see what you qualify for.

scholarships.gov.in Apply on official portal ↗
🏦

PM-Vidyalakshmi (Education loans)

Collateral-free, guarantor-free education loans up to ₹10 lakh at accredited institutions, with interest subsidy for eligible income groups.

vidyalakshmi.co.in Apply on official portal ↗
🏅

National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship

A stipend for meritorious students from economically weaker sections to continue Class 9–12 in a government school.

scholarships.gov.in Apply on official portal ↗
🛠️

PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana (Skill training)

Free, short-term skill-training courses for youth, with a certificate and placement assistance on completion.

myscheme.gov.in Search on official portal ↗
🏠

Income-Based — Housing & Health

Housing subsidies, health cover and essentials support, with eligibility usually based on your household income category (EWS/LIG/MIG or BPL).

🏡

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY)

Interest subsidy and financial assistance for buying or building a home, for households in the EWS, LIG and MIG income categories.

pmaymis.gov.in Apply on official portal ↗
🏥

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

Free hospitalisation cover up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for eligible lower-income households, at empanelled hospitals nationwide.

pmjay.gov.in Check eligibility ↗
🔥

PM Ujjwala Yojana

A free LPG gas connection for women from low-income households, to replace unsafe traditional cooking fuel.

myscheme.gov.in Search on official portal ↗
🧓

Age-Based — Senior Citizens & Youth

Pension and savings schemes for senior residents, and skill/employment schemes for younger residents just starting out.

💰

Atal Pension Yojana & PM Jan Suraksha schemes

A guaranteed monthly pension after age 60 for anyone aged 18–40 who enrols and contributes, plus low-cost life and accident insurance.

jansuraksha.gov.in Learn more ↗
🧑‍🦳

Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension

A monthly pension for senior citizens from BPL households, as part of the National Social Assistance Programme.

myscheme.gov.in Search on official portal ↗
🎒

PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana (Youth)

Skill certification and placement support aimed at young residents entering the workforce for the first time.

myscheme.gov.in Search on official portal ↗
Please note: this page is a resident convenience, not an official government service. Bhargavi Housing Society doesn't process applications, verify eligibility, or collect any documents for these schemes — every "Apply / Learn more" button opens the real, official government site, where you should always check the current eligibility, required documents and benefit amounts before applying. Applying for every scheme listed here is free — never pay any person or agent claiming to guarantee approval.
